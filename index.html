Questions

Before you ask

Anything else? hello@bejucreative.digital — we reply within 24 hours.

How much does short-form video editing cost?

Short-form edits start at $27 for a single video, $60 for three, and $75 for five — $15 per edit at the five-video tier. Long-form is priced per finished video, from $75. Prices vary with the length of the source footage; these are averages for 15–40 second reels.

Do you offer a free sample edit?

Yes. Say yes to a free custom clip and we’ll pick a suitable moment from your existing footage, or ask you for a link. No payment, card details or purchase commitment is required. The one-time sample is separate from any paid package you choose afterwards.

How fast is the turnaround?

Standard short-form packages are delivered within 24 hours after payment, usable footage and the brief are confirmed. For a guaranteed package, we also agree the baseline before payment. If an unusually large order or significant cleanup needs more time, we agree that upfront. One revision round is included.

What happens if the edits don't perform?

Our performance guarantee covers every qualifying paid short-form package. If its average first-30-day organic views per clip are below twice the baseline agreed before payment, we refund the full amount paid for that package. For example, a $75 package receives a $75 refund. Long-form editing and launch films are excluded.

How do you calculate my baseline and the 2× result?

Before payment, we note the public view counts, as shown on the platform, of your last 10 clips on one agreed account that are at least 30 days old, or all of them if you have fewer than 10. Their average is your baseline, and you confirm it before paying. Your delivered package is measured on each clip’s views in its first 30 days after posting: total views divided by the number of delivered clips. A 1,000-view baseline means a 2,000-view target per clip on average. Each individual clip does not need to double; for a one-clip order, that clip is the package average. If you have fewer than three clips that are at least 30 days old, the guarantee does not apply, and we tell you so before payment.

When does the 30-day window start, and how must I post?

Publish every delivered clip on the agreed account and platform within 14 days of delivery. For larger packages, a written posting schedule agreed before payment replaces that deadline. Each clip’s measurement window starts when you post it, not when we deliver it. Keep each clip public and unchanged, with no paid promotion, throughout its first 30 days. The package result is final once the last posted clip completes its 30-day window.

How do I request a performance refund?

Email hello@bejucreative.digital within seven calendar days after the last clip completes its 30-day window. Include your order details, links and posting dates for every delivered clip, plus platform analytics showing each clip’s organic views for its first 30 days. We compare the package average with your recorded baseline and refund the full package payment if the conditions are met and the target was missed.

What if I want a different editing style?

One revision round is included to address feedback within the agreed brief. A style preference or change of mind alone does not trigger this performance refund. The guarantee is tied to the measured 2× target, not subjective satisfaction. This does not limit any rights that apply under law.

Does the guarantee continue when I order again?

Yes, for qualifying short-form orders. Your agreed baseline stays fixed for three months from the baseline agreement date, then we reassess it before further orders. Each package is evaluated separately against the baseline recorded for that order. We do not promise to double the previous month repeatedly, and a later baseline change does not alter an existing order’s target.

What footage do you need from me?

Whatever you already have — a podcast recording, a coaching call, or a talk to camera. We cut it into short clips. You don't need to film anything new, write scripts or prepare shot lists.

Which platforms do you format for?

Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn. Every clip is exported and sized for each platform, with captions burned in.

Do you make launch videos for SaaS and apps?

Yes — short launch videos for SaaS products, apps and other product launches. They're quoted per project: tell us about your launch and we'll reply with a quote within 24 hours.
